Kynbat

A visual identity system for a built-environment brand, designed to balance architectural precision with a human, long-term point of view.

ScopeVisual identity
DeliverablesLogo, palette, typography, collateral
ToolFigma
Design direction

Structure, space and clarity.

The system combines architectural forms, an editorial type treatment and a restrained blue-grey palette with a precise red accent. It gives the brand a clear foundation across physical and digital communication.

Identity

A geometric logo system references built space while remaining legible at small scales.

Typography

Editorial display type and practical sans-serif copy create a confident hierarchy.

Applications

Cards, social content and document templates extend the identity into everyday brand touchpoints.
