The Challenge

Translating ancestral wellness into a digital experience

Shaila, a solo Ayurvedic practitioner based in Béziers, France, needed a website that would reflect the warmth and intentionality of her practice. The challenge was to create a luxury digital presence that communicates trust, positions her services as premium, and converts visitors into bookings — all while honoring the depth of Bengali Ayurvedic tradition.

Premium Luxury positioning for a solo practice
Trust Build confidence before the first visit
Convert Turn visitors into booked clients
The Solution

Editorial luxury meets conversion design

I designed and built a complete website in Webflow that balances editorial elegance with a clear conversion path. The site uses a three-font typography system (Cormorant Garamond, Playfair Display, Inter) that signals luxury while remaining readable. Every section builds trust — from the philosophy statement to client testimonials — before guiding visitors into a transparent 3-step booking funnel.

01

Editorial Typography System

A three-font hierarchy — Cormorant Garamond for headlines, Playfair Display for accent text, and Inter for body — creates a luxury editorial feel that matches the warmth and refinement of the Ayurvedic practice.

02

Service Cards & Transparent Pricing

Clear service cards for consultations, massages, and immersive experiences — paired with transparent pricing (Abhyanga 60€, Shirodhara 60€, Bol Kansu 35€) that eliminates friction before booking.

1. Planifier — Choose a time slot 2. Questionnaire — Tailor the session 3. Paiement — Secure booking
03

3-Step Booking Funnel

A conversion-focused booking flow that reduces anxiety: schedule online, fill a short pre-session questionnaire, then pay securely. Each step is explained upfront so first-time visitors know exactly what to expect.

Process

From brand positioning to live website

This project required understanding both the wellness industry's visual language and the conversion patterns that turn browsers into booked clients.

1

Brand Discovery

Worked with Shaila to understand her practice philosophy, target audience (French-speaking women seeking holistic wellness), and the unique blend of Bengali tradition and modern Ayurvedic care.

2

Visual Direction & Typography

Developed a luxury editorial direction with warm earth tones and a three-font system that signals premium quality while remaining approachable and soothing.

3

Content Architecture

Structured the page flow to build trust first — philosophy, then services, then pricing, then testimonials, then FAQ — before the booking CTA. Every section addresses a visitor's objection or question.

4

Webflow Build & Launch

Built the entire site in Webflow with responsive design, smooth interactions, and CMS-ready structure — empowering Shaila to update content independently after launch.

Outcome

Key takeaways

A premium digital presence that matches the practice

The editorial design and warm visual language communicate luxury, trust, and intentionality — aligning the digital experience with the in-person Ayurvedic treatments.

Trust-first content strategy drives conversions

Client testimonials, transparent pricing, an FAQ that addresses objections, and a clear 3-step booking process reduce friction for first-time visitors.

Client independence with Webflow

Built in Webflow so Shaila can update services, pricing, and testimonials herself — no developer needed for day-to-day content changes.
