About

I'm Otmane Allaoui — a Product Designer based in Morocco with 4+ years of experience in SaaS, fintech, mobility, and AI.

I specialize in UX/UI, design systems, prototyping, and data visualization. I've evolved MVPs into high-fidelity, complex products — always keeping user-centered design at the core.

Today I combine product design expertise with vibe coding — using AI tools like Claude and Cursor alongside FlutterFlow and Tailwind to go from idea to live product. I believe the future belongs to designers who can build.
