The Challenge

Tracking influencers is manual and fragmented

Marketing teams managing influencer campaigns often rely on spreadsheets, scattered tools, and manual processes to track performance. There was no unified platform to monitor influencer effectiveness, measure campaign results, and make data-driven decisions — all in one place.

Manual Fragmented tracking across tools
No AI Lacking smart analysis capabilities
Slow Inefficient monitoring workflows
The Solution

A data-driven monitoring platform

I designed a unified platform that centralizes influencer tracking, campaign performance monitoring, and AI-powered analysis into one intuitive interface — improving monitoring efficiency by 30%.

01

Persona-Driven Workflows

Created detailed personas and mapped user workflows to ensure the platform addressed real pain points for marketing teams managing influencer campaigns.

02

Data-Driven Interface

Designed a performance dashboard with clear data visualizations to track influencer metrics, campaign results, and engagement data at a glance.

03

AI Analysis Modules

Integrated AI-powered analysis modules into the prototype to surface insights on influencer performance and campaign effectiveness automatically.

Process

How I designed it

This project was part of my work at Evead Group, where I led design initiatives for AI and data-driven products.

1

Personas & Research

Created detailed personas to understand different user types — from campaign managers to brand strategists — and their specific monitoring needs.

2

Workflow Mapping

Mapped end-to-end user workflows for campaign tracking, influencer evaluation, and performance reporting to ensure a streamlined experience.

3

Data-Driven UI Design

Designed the interface with a focus on data visualization and clarity — making complex influencer metrics easy to scan and act on.

4

High-Fidelity Prototyping

Delivered high-fidelity interactive prototypes in Figma with integrated AI analysis module concepts, ready for development handoff.

Outcome

Key takeaways

30% improvement in monitoring efficiency

The data-driven interface and streamlined workflows significantly reduced the time teams spent on manual influencer tracking.

AI-integrated prototypes

Delivered high-fidelity prototypes with AI analysis modules that demonstrated how smart insights could drive better campaign decisions.

Persona-driven design decisions

Every design choice was grounded in real user personas and validated workflows, ensuring the platform addressed actual pain points.
